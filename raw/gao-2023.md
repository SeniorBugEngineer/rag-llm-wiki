## Abstract（原文）

> Large Language Models (LLMs) showcase impressive capabilities but encounter challenges like hallucination, outdated knowledge, and non-transparent, untraceable reasoning processes. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) has emerged as a promising solution by incorporating knowledge from external databases. This enhances the accuracy and credibility of the generation, particularly for knowledge-intensive tasks, and allows for continuous knowledge updates and integration of domain-specific information. RAG synergistically merges LLMs' intrinsic knowledge with the vast, dynamic repositories of external databases. This comprehensive review paper offers a detailed examination of the progression of RAG paradigms, encompassing the Naive RAG, the Advanced RAG, and the Modular RAG. It meticulously scrutinizes the tripartite foundation of RAG frameworks, which includes the retrieval, the generation and the augmentation techniques. The paper highlights the state-of-the-art technologies embedded in each of these critical components, providing a profound understanding of the advancements in RAG systems. Furthermore, this paper introduces up-to-date evaluation framework and benchmark. At the end, this article delineates the challenges currently faced and points out prospective avenues for research and development.

## 內文擷取筆記（[章節] 事實；數字照原文）

- [II-A Naive RAG] 「Retrieve-Read」三步：Indexing（清理 PDF/HTML 等 → 切 chunk → embedding → 向量資料庫）、Retrieval（用同一模型編碼查詢，取 top-K 相似 chunk）、Generation（查詢 + chunk 組成 prompt 給 LLM）。
- [II-A 缺點] 檢索精確率／召回率不足；生成仍會幻覺；多來源重複、拼接不連貫；一次檢索對複雜問題不夠；生成器可能只是照抄檢索內容。
- [II-B Advanced RAG] Pre-retrieval：索引最佳化（sliding window、更細切分、加 metadata、混合檢索）、查詢最佳化（query rewriting / transformation / expansion）。Post-retrieval：rerank（把最相關內容放到 prompt 邊緣）、context compression（如 LLMLingua、RECOMP）。
- [II-C Modular RAG] 新模組：Search、RAG-Fusion、Memory、Routing、Predict、Task Adapter；新模式：Rewrite-Retrieve-Read、Generate-Read、Recite-Read、Hybrid retrieval、HyDE、DSP、ITER-RETGEN、FLARE、Self-RAG。模組可替換、重組，不限於先檢索再生成。
- [II-D RAG vs Fine-tuning] RAG 適合動態知識、可即時更新、可解釋性高但延遲較高；FT 適合客製模型行為、風格、格式，但更新知識需重新訓練；兩者可互補。引述研究：RAG 在既有與全新知識上都穩定優於無監督 fine-tuning。
- [Abstract] LLM 的挑戰：hallucination、outdated knowledge、non-transparent, untraceable reasoning；RAG 結合 LLM 內在知識與外部資料庫。
- [III Chunking] 常見固定大小 100／256／512 tokens；大 chunk 上下文多但雜訊與成本高，小 chunk 雜訊少但可能缺上下文；Small2Big：用句子檢索、把前後文一起交給 LLM。
- [V Augmentation process] Iterative retrieval（檢索與生成交替，如 ITER-RETGEN）、Recursive retrieval（逐步細化查詢、拆子問題，如 IRCoT）、Adaptive retrieval（由 LLM 決定要不要／何時檢索，如 FLARE、Self-RAG）。
- [VI Evaluation] 品質分數：context relevance、answer faithfulness、answer relevance；能力：noise robustness、negative rejection、information integration、counterfactual robustness。作者指出這些指標尚未標準化。
