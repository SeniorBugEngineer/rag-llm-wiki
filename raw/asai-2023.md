## Abstract（原文）

> Despite their remarkable capabilities, large language models (LLMs) often produce responses containing factual inaccuracies due to their sole reliance on the parametric knowledge they encapsulate. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), an ad hoc approach that augments LMs with retrieval of relevant knowledge, decreases such issues. However, indiscriminately retrieving and incorporating a fixed number of retrieved passages, regardless of whether retrieval is necessary, or passages are relevant, diminishes LM versatility or can lead to unhelpful response generation. We introduce a new framework called Self-Reflective Retrieval-Augmented Generation (Self-RAG) that enhances an LM's quality and factuality through retrieval and self-reflection. Our framework trains a single arbitrary LM that adaptively retrieves passages on-demand, and generates and reflects on retrieved passages and its own generations using special tokens, called reflection tokens. Generating reflection tokens makes the LM controllable during the inference phase, enabling it to tailor its behavior to diverse task requirements. Experiments show that Self-RAG (7B and 13B parameters) significantly outperforms state-of-the-art LLMs and retrieval-augmented models on a diverse set of tasks. Specifically, Self-RAG outperforms ChatGPT and retrieval-augmented Llama2-chat on Open-domain QA, reasoning and fact verification tasks, and it shows significant gains in improving factuality and citation accuracy for long-form generations relative to these models.

## 內文擷取筆記（[章節] 事實；數字照原文）

- [Table 1 Reflection tokens] Retrieve（yes／no／continue：要不要呼叫檢索器）；IsRel（relevant／irrelevant：檢索段落是否有用）；IsSup（fully／partially／no support：回答是否被段落支持）；IsUse（5–1：回答對問題有多有用）。
- [Algorithm 1 Inference] 先預測 Retrieve；若 yes，檢索段落，對每段平行判斷 IsRel 並各自生成候選；再對候選預測 IsSup、IsUse；依三者排序選出最佳片段。若 no，直接生成並預測 IsUse。
- [3.2 Training] 用 GPT-4 標註 reflection tokens，蒸餾成 critic（Llama 2-7B）；critic 離線把 tokens 與段落插入訓練語料，再以一般 next-token 目標訓練 generator（Llama 2 7B／13B）。推論時不需要 critic。約 150k 筆 instruction-output。
- [3.3 Inference] Retrieve=yes 的機率超過門檻（多數任務 0.2）才檢索；segment 層級 beam search 以生成機率加上 IsRel／IsSup／IsUse 的加權分數排序，權重可在推論時調整（預設 1.0／1.0／0.5）。
- [Table 2] PopQA 正確率：ChatGPT 29.3、Ret-ChatGPT 50.8、Ret-Llama2-chat-13B 51.8、Self-RAG 7B 54.9、13B 55.8；PubHealth：ChatGPT 70.1、Self-RAG 13B 74.5；Biography FactScore：ChatGPT 71.8、Self-RAG 7B 81.2；ASQA 引用 precision／recall：Self-RAG 13B 70.3／71.3、Ret-ChatGPT 65.1／76.6。
- [摘要主張] 固定檢索 k 份段落、不管需不需要，會降低模型多用途性並可能產生無用回答。
